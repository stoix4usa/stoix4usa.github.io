The Onward and Upward Climb

A Returning Missionary’s Homecoming Plan · 27 pages

The plan itself, in seven sections, built for a phone. Every scripture is a live link. Start here; everything below travels with it.

The What, Why, How, and When of Personal Preparation

1

Feasting on the Words of Christ — The Doctrine

4 pages

The daily ten step discipline to receive revelation and the Feast Log — the morning routine and the record of it. One line a day to capture in the log is the whole discipline. It is the practice whose loss predicts every other loss.

→ The Feast Log — a five-tab workbook. Download it and keep it.

2

The Practice Check-in — Staying Safely on the Path

1 page

Nine practices and the cadence that keeps them real. Five connect you UPWARD to God. Four connect you OUTWARD, to His children. Ask one honest question each week: Which wall slipped in the least, and what is the smallest action that restores it?

3

Wisdom — Lessons from the Past

25 pages

Where the twelve walls in Part 2 come from and why you should trust them. The war chapters, the kings of Israel, and one pattern found in both. Every scripture is a live link, so you can check every claim yourself. Study the terrain so you are prepared for what’s around the corner.

4

The Twelve Walls — Your Own Reading

4 pages

The working sheet that goes with Wisdom. Print it, mark it, date it. Then do it again in six months. One reading is a snapshot. The second shows the direction of your travel.

5

Math That Doesn’t Work

13 pages · Part 3 of the Climb

It is the third part of the plan above, so you already have it. It also travels on its own, for someone who needs this perspective and nothing else.

Additional Resources for your Climb

6

Life Skills — a Church-sponsored Self-Reliance course

12 weeks

Written for older youth and young single adults, to build the practical, spiritual, and temporal skills that carry you into work, school, and adult life. It gathers three courses into one — Personal Finances, Find a Better Job, and Education for Better Work — built on My Foundation for Self-Reliance. Small groups, in person or online. If no group is meeting near you, you can work through it on your own, or with friends.

7

Additional Self-Reliance courses

8–12 weeks

Emotional Resilience. Starting and Growing My Business. Same shape: a small group, a facilitator, eight to twelve weeks, in person or online. Pick one as you build out the first year — and the second, and the third.

One caution, and it comes from Part 3. These are items. Good ones, and worth adding. REMEMBER: an item is not a multiplier. Take the course under the purpose you chose, not instead of it.

Five documents and two courses. Nothing to download in a bundle — open what you need, when you need it.
