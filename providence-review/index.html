The Thanksgiving Providence Review

A meditation for taking a reading of one’s standing before the God of the Universe, and acknowledging His merciful and loving hand in one’s affairs.

Built from Elder Moisés Villanueva’s October 2026 general conference talk, “Prove Me Now Herewith.” On its face the talk is about tithing. Underneath, it is a method: take the blessing you expected and did not get, and turn it into an inventory of the blessings you did get.

What the talk does

  1. Names the law and its promise. Obey the law; receive the blessing. (Malachi 3:10; D&C 130:20–21)
  2. Remembers who paid first. His mother, newly baptized and raising eight children alone, paid a full tithe anyway.
  3. Faces a disappointment honestly. A faithful sister paid her tithing and still missed the promotion she hoped for. He did not argue with her. He gave her a task: make a list.
  4. Makes the list. Steady work, family health, a home kept safe, the means for an education — and quieter gifts, like gratitude and knowing the Spirit in big decisions.
  5. Looks for what lasted. A sister’s small propane tank kept going long past reason. (Deuteronomy 29:5)
  6. Gives first, then sees. The widow of Zarephath baked Elijah’s cake from her last meal, and her meal and oil did not run out. (1 Kings 17:13–16)
  7. Testifies. God knows what we need before we ask. (Matthew 6:8)

His key line is that faithful living often brings “significant but subtle blessings.” The sister was not missing blessings. She had never counted them. The cure was a pen.

The review

Give it about 90 minutes, in six blocks of 15 minutes. Or take one movement a day through Thanksgiving week. You need a pen, last year’s journal or calendar, and the scriptures linked below. Each set of questions starts wide and ends with a yes-or-no.

Before you begin · 2 minutes

Clear the desk.

One minute: write down everything on your mind that is competing for attention. It is safe now; you can let it go.

One minute: write down what you hope the Lord will show you today.

Movement I

The terms I live under

Prove me now herewith, saith the Lord of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it.Malachi 3:10
  1. What has the Lord asked of me this year?
  2. Which of those laws did I keep when it cost me something?
  3. Which promises tied to those laws am I counting on?
  4. Yes or no: Did I keep each of them fully?
Law I livedWhat it costThe promise

Movement II

Who paid the first price

Who am I, saith the Lord, that have promised and have not fulfilled?D&C 58:31
  1. Whose faith am I living on?
  2. What did their obedience cost them, and what did it give me?
  3. Yes or no: Have I thanked them, or thanked the Lord for them, this year?

Movement III

The blessing that did not come

  1. Where did I expect the Lord to answer one way, and He answered another way — or not yet?
  2. How did I decide what the answer ought to be?
  3. Yes or no: Am I willing to accept His way and His timing on this one?

Write it plainly. Do not argue with it yet.

Movement IV

The ledger

  1. Where has His hand been in my affairs this year?
  2. Which of these did I almost not count?
  3. Yes or no: Is there anything on this page I had been calling luck?

Movement V

What lasted longer than it should have

Your clothes are not waxen old upon you, and thy shoe is not waxen old upon thy foot.Deuteronomy 29:5
  1. What in my life has worn better than reason predicts?
  2. What would losing it have cost me?
  3. Yes or no: Do I give Him credit for it?

Think of a body, a marriage, a business, a vehicle, an appliance, a friendship, energy for the work.

Movement VI

The widow’s cake

And the barrel of meal wasted not, neither did the cruse of oil fail, according to the word of the Lord, which he spake by Elijah.1 Kings 17:16
  1. Where did I give first this year, before I could see the return?
  2. Where is the Lord asking me now to bake the cake first?
  3. Yes or no: Will I do it before I see the meal and the oil?

Movement VII

My standing: taking the reading

“Prove” runs both ways. In Malachi the Lord invites us to prove Him. In the book of Abraham He says He will prove us:

And we will prove them herewith, to see if they will do all things whatsoever the Lord their God shall command them.Abraham 3:25

A reading is dated and taken again. Today’s date: ____________. If you have last year’s reading, lay it beside this one.

  1. Looking at Movements I through VI, what does this year say about how faithful He has been?
  2. What does it say about how faithful I have been?
  3. What one part of my covenants will I keep more fully in the coming year?
  4. Compared with my last reading, which way is the needle moving?
  5. Yes or no: Do I believe He knew these needs before I asked? (Matthew 6:8)

Keep this page. It is the starting point for your next reading.

Then kneel, and give the ledger back to Him in prayer, one line at a time.
